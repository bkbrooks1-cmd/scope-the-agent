## Labels

**Script**: no decision. Same input, same output. Build a tool, not an agent.
**Agent**: the rule is stated, "wrong" is stated, and the owner accepts an occasional wrong answer. All three, or it is not an agent.
**Human**: the rule cannot be stated, or the step decides an approval or an outside commitment, or wrong is unacceptable even once, or nobody knows what wrong costs.
